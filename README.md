# Hi there, I'm a Cookie Compliance & Consent Specialist 👋

I bridge the gap between web development and data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, ePrivacy Directive). I actively audit digital platforms—focusing on fintech, e-commerce, and fast-growing startups—to detect illicit tracking, non-consented cookie drops, and broken consent architectures. 

Together with my technical team, I help businesses mitigate regulatory risks and build trust with their users through rock-solid consent management.

---

### 🛡️ What I Do
* **Automated & Manual Privacy Auditing:** Scanning website background network traffic to ensure no tracking pixels or cookies fire before explicit user consent.
* **Consent Architecture Evaluation:** Dissecting cookie banners, preference centers, and privacy policies for dark patterns and compliance gaps.
* **Data Logging & Risk Reporting:** Compiling structured compliance data and technical proof to present actionable risk assessments to C-level executives (CEOs, CTOs).
* **Implementation Oversight:** Partnering with engineering teams to correctly deploy, configure, and validate consent tools like Google Tag Manager (GTM), Consent Mode v2, and leading Consent Management Platforms (CMPs).
